Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

SubScript is designed to work without collecting your personal data. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the App.

1. Summary

  • No account, no registration, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • Your history, files, folders and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Developer does not receive, see or store your links, files or texts.

2. Data stored on your device

The App stores locally, in its private storage: the cleaned texts you processed, translations you chose to save, titles, source links, small thumbnail images, folders, language preferences, downloaded language models and fonts, and app settings. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to save or share a file. You can delete individual items, folders, models or all history in the App at any time; uninstalling the App deletes its storage (files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them).

3. When the App uses the internet

The App connects to the internet only when needed for a feature you use:

  1. Getting subtitles from a link: the App sends requests directly from your device to the video platform (for example YouTube) to read the title, thumbnail and subtitle data. That platform receives your IP address and the request, as with any visit, under its own privacy policy.
  2. Downloading language models or fonts: the App downloads them from their providers (for example Google ML Kit model servers or font hosts). These providers may receive your IP address.
  3. Translation on Windows and macOS: on computers, translation uses an online translation service. The text you choose to translate is sent to that service and handled under its own privacy policy. The App asks for your consent before the first use. On Android and iOS, translation happens on your device after the language model is downloaded.

No information is sent to servers operated by the Developer.

4. Permissions

The App only asks for what features need: internet access, and access to the files or folders you choose when importing, saving or sharing. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or other files.

5. Sharing

When you tap Share, your device's share menu sends the file to the app or person you choose. What happens next is governed by that app or person.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

7. Security

Your data stays in the App's private storage protected by your device's operating system. Keep your device secured with a lock screen; anyone with access to your unlocked device may see your history.

8. Your rights

Because the Developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side; you control all your data directly in the App. For questions about third-party services, contact those providers.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The new version applies from its publication in the App or on the website.
